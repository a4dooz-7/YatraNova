Pokhara Adventures

Explore the serene lakes, mystical caves, and towering peaks of Nepal's tourism capital.

Phewa Lake

The heartbeat of Pokhara, this freshwater lake mirrors the Annapurna range on its glass-like surface. Whether you're paddling a colorful 'Doonga' or watching the sunset from a lakeside café, its beauty is unmatched.

Major Activity: Boating to Tal Barahi Temple
Best Feature: Inverted reflection of Mt. Machhapuchhre

Begnas Lake

For those seeking silence over crowds, Begnas Lake offers a pristine, untouched escape. Surrounded by lush green hills and terraced fields, it provides a authentic glimpse into the tranquil Nepali countryside.

Major Activity: Freshwater fishing and quiet paddling
Best Feature: Crystal clear turquoise waters and solitude

Sarangkot: Sunrise Spectacle

Perched at 1,600m, Sarangkot offers a front-row seat to the greatest show on earth. Watch the sky turn from deep violet to burning orange as the sun kisses the peaks of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges.

Major Activity: Early morning photography and hiking
Best Feature: 360-degree Himalayan dawn panorama

Sarangkot: Aerial Paragliding

Take a leap of faith from the cliffs of Sarangkot. Soar alongside eagles and vultures as you glide over Phewa Lake with the massive Himalayas providing a backdrop that looks almost surreal from the air.

Major Activity: Tandem Paragliding adventures
Best Feature: Bird's eye view of the entire Pokhara Valley

Pumdikot Religious Area

Home to the colossal 51-foot tall statue of Lord Shiva, Pumdikot has quickly become a spiritual landmark. The hilltop location offers a divine atmosphere paired with sweeping views of the city below.

Major Activity: Pilgrimage and hilltop sightseeing
Best Feature: Massive Shiva Statue and panoramic deck

Mahendra Cave

Descend into the cool depths of this limestone wonder. Named after King Mahendra, the cave is a labyrinth of natural rock formations, including a sacred stone lingam dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Major Activity: Spelunking and cave exploration
Best Feature: Unique stalactite and stalagmite formations

Davis Fall (Patale Chhango)

Nature’s disappearing act: Davis Fall is a powerful cascade that vanishes into a deep, mysterious underground tunnel. During the monsoon, the roar of the water is a humbling reminder of nature's raw energy.

Major Activity: Sightseeing and visiting the 'Wishing Pool'
Best Feature: The dramatic underground waterfall tunnel

Mt. Machhapuchhre (Fishtail)

The "Fishtail" mountain is the silent guardian of Pokhara. Sacred and unclimbed, its unique double-peak silhouette is the most iconic landmark of the region, visible from almost every corner of the city.

Major Activity: Mountain flights and base camp trekking
Best Feature: Iconic 'Fishtail' summit shape (6,993m)

World Peace Pagoda (Shanti Stupa)

Standing as a white beacon on Anadu Hill, this monument is one of 80 peace pagodas worldwide. It offers a meditative atmosphere and perhaps the best balanced view of the lake, city, and mountains.

Major Activity: Hiking from Lakeside or forest walking
Best Feature: Serene Buddhist architecture and 360-degree views

Bindhyabasini Temple

Located in the old bazaar, this is Pokhara’s oldest and most revered Hindu temple. Dedicated to Goddess Durga, the temple courtyard is a vibrant center of local culture and religious devotion.

Major Activity: Observing traditional rituals and pujas
Best Feature: Stunning mountain views from the temple heights
